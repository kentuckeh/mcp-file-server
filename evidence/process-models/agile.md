# Agile Model

> Reviewed in Session 2. See INDEX.md for the full list.

## Definition
- An iterative, people-oriented development model emphasizing rapid release cycles, flexible adaptation to change, and continuous customer feedback.

## When to Use
- Best suited for dynamic projects with uncertain or fast-evolving requirements, rapid delivery demands, and close stakeholder collaboration.

## Pros
- Highly adaptive to evolving customer priorities and market demands throughout the project lifecycle.
- Delivers functioning, tested software at regular, short intervals with transparent progress visibility.

## Cons
- Challenging to forecast overall project duration, budget, and final scope in early project stages.
- Requires high stakeholder involvement, experienced self-organizing teams, and disciplined communication.
